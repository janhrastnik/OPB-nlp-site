UFO Sightings

UFOs, or Unidentified Flying Objects, have fascinated humans for centuries, with sightings dating back to ancient cave paintings and historical texts describing strange lights or flying objects. The modern UFO era began in 1947 when pilot Kenneth Arnold reported seeing disk-shaped crafts near Mount Rainier, coining the term "flying saucer." That same year, the famous Roswell incident sparked widespread speculation about government cover-ups and extraterrestrial visitors. Since then, UFOs have remained a topic of mystery and debate, with governments and scientists occasionally releasing reports but offering few definitive answers.
